\subsection{Discussion}
1. \textbf{Superiority of Order Encoding:} Due to natural Unit Propagation along the chain $L(u, i) \implies L(u, i+1)$, Order Encoding prunes the decision tree effectively, outperforming Pairwise Encoding by approximately $\approx 5\times$ in execution speed.
2. \textbf{Efficiency of Incremental SAT:} Reusing solver state and learned clauses allows Incremental SAT to maintain search stability when approaching phase transition regions near optimal bounds.
