\section{Conclusion}
This study successfully implements, optimizes, and evaluates a complete SAT-based solving framework for the Graph Bandwidth Minimization Problem. Empirical evidence confirms that \textbf{Order Encoding combined with Incremental SAT} provides the most efficient and scalable solver architecture for sparse graphs.
